听记隐私政策

更新日期：【上架日期】

听记是一款用 Apple Watch 录音、在 iPhone 上整理成文字和摘要的软件。我们（开发者）没有自己的服务器，不需要注册账号，收集不到、也看不到你的任何录音、文字和个人信息。

1. 数据保存在哪里

  • 录音、文字稿、说话人名称、摘要，都只保存在你自己的 Apple Watch 和 iPhone 上。
  • 手表的录音通过苹果的 WatchConnectivity 直接传给配对的 iPhone，不经过 iCloud，也不经过我们。
  • 你设置的模型密钥只保存在 iPhone 的系统钥匙串里，不随 iCloud 同步。
  • 删除 App 会删除上述全部数据。录音可以在设置里选择保留 30 天、90 天或永久。

2. 什么情况下数据会离开你的手机

只有你主动开启下列功能时，数据才会发送出去，而且是从你的 iPhone 直接发送到你自己选择的服务商，使用你自己的账号和密钥：

功能发送什么发送给谁
生成每日摘要当天的文字稿（不含录音）你在设置里选的模型服务（DeepSeek，或你自定义的兼容 OpenAI 接口的服务）
导出给 AI / 分享你选中的文字记录你在系统分享面板里选的 App

这些服务商如何处理数据，以它们各自的隐私政策为准。打开设置里的"只在本地处理"后，任何内容都不会离开你的手机。

3. 购买

  • "3 天免费试用"和"永久解锁"通过苹果 App Store 内购完成，付款由苹果处理。我们拿不到你的付款信息、Apple 账号或姓名。
  • App 只在你的手机上向 App Store 查询"这个 Apple 账号有没有买过/试用过听记"，用来决定是否解锁摘要等功能。

4. 我们不做的事

  • 不收集设备标识、位置、联系人、使用统计；App 内没有第三方统计、广告或崩溃上报工具。
  • 不出售、不共享任何数据。

5. 录音他人

录音前请遵守你所在地关于录音的法律。部分国家和地区要求获得所有在场者同意。手表录音时，系统会显示麦克风指示。

6. 未成年人

听记不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

TingJi Privacy Policy

Last updated: [launch date]

TingJi records with your Apple Watch and turns recordings into transcripts and summaries on your iPhone. We (the developer) run no servers and require no account. We do not collect, and cannot see, any of your recordings, transcripts or personal information.

1. Where your data is stored

  • Recordings, transcripts, speaker names and summaries are stored only on your own Apple Watch and iPhone.
  • Recordings move from the Watch to the paired iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity. They do not go through iCloud or through us.
  • The model API key you enter is stored only in the iPhone's system Keychain and does not sync to iCloud.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of the above. You can keep recordings for 30 days, 90 days or forever in Settings.

2. When data leaves your iPhone

Data is sent only when you use the features below, directly from your iPhone to the provider you chose, with your own account and key:

FeatureWhat is sentSent to
Daily summaryThat day's transcript text (no audio)The model service you chose in Settings (DeepSeek, or a custom OpenAI-compatible service)
Export / ShareThe text you selectedThe app you pick in the system share sheet

How those providers handle data is governed by their own privacy policies. With "Local only" turned on in Settings, nothing leaves your iPhone.

3. Purchases

  • The "3-day Trial" and "Lifetime Unlock" are App Store in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, Apple ID or name.
  • The app asks the App Store, on your iPhone, whether your Apple ID has purchased or started the trial, only to decide whether to unlock features.

4. What we don't do

  • No device identifiers, location, contacts or usage analytics. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

5. Recording other people

Follow the recording laws where you are. Some countries and regions require consent from everyone present. The system shows a microphone indicator while the Watch is recording.

6. Children

TingJi is not directed at children under 13.
