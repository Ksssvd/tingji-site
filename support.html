听记 · 帮助与支持

常见问题

  • 录音传不到手机？ 让手机和手表靠近、都打开听记，录音会自动补传。首页会显示手表上还有几段没传。
  • 怎么生成摘要？ 在设置里填写你自己的模型密钥（推荐 DeepSeek），再在首页点某一天的"当天摘要"。
  • 换手机后购买还在吗？ 在设置 → 购买 → 恢复购买，用同一个 Apple 账号即可恢复。
  • 数据存在哪里？ 只在你的手表和手机上，开发者没有服务器。

TingJi · Help & Support

FAQ

  • Recordings not reaching the iPhone? Keep the iPhone and Watch close with TingJi open on both; recordings resume uploading automatically. The home screen shows how many are still on the Watch.
  • How do I get a daily summary? Enter your own model API key in Settings (DeepSeek recommended), then open a day's summary on the home screen.
  • Moved to a new iPhone? Settings → Purchase → Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.
  • Where is my data? Only on your Watch and iPhone. The developer runs no servers.
